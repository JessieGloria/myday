# My Day

Your personal planner: Today (plan, to do, to buy), Calendar (roster, leave, Sabah holidays).
Money (step 2): income, spending, savings, budgets, bills, card owed, screenshot to expense.
Goals and Learn come in step 3.

## Put it online (same as Apolaki)

1. On GitHub, create a new repository called `myday`.
2. Upload everything in this folder (keep the `icons` folder).
3. On Vercel, choose Add New → Project, pick `myday`, and press Deploy. No settings needed.
4. Open the link on your iPhone in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Sign in with your OMFS Desk email and password.

## Files

- `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`, `money.js`: the app
- `config.js`: your Supabase connection
- `manifest.json`, `sw.js`, `icons/`: home screen install
